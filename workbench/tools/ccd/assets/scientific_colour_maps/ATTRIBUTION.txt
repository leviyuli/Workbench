The first 32 colors of the normalized batlowS table from Scientific colour maps
version 8.0.1 are embedded, in the original categorical order, in plot_style.py.
The table was supplied with the local pyplot-setup skill. No package or external
palette file is needed at runtime.

Crameri, F. (2018), Scientific colour maps, Zenodo,
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1243862
https://www.fabiocrameri.ch/colourmaps/

Crameri, F., G. E. Shephard, and P. J. Heron (2020), The misuse of colour in
science communication, Nature Communications, 11, 5444.
